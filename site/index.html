An applied AI lab

Strong, flexible, connected.

We build robots that work over private 5G, keep their reflexes on board and think with an LLM at the edge. Along the way we make the tools we wish existed: OCR that reads Vietnamese properly, and checks that keep AI agents from claiming what they cannot back.

What we're working on

Robotics

Robots over private 5G

ROS 2 across a real 5G radio link. Reflex control stays on the robot, an LLM planner runs on a GPU at the network edge, and every hop is measured.

Language

Vietnamese OCR

A small tone mark changes the meaning of a Vietnamese word. We build OCR that gets them right, with the accuracy measured, not assumed.

AI tooling

Honest AI agents

Checks that stop an agent from stating facts it cannot back, and exercises for questioning claims and decisions before acting on them.

Early days. The lab is running; public demos and code are on their way.
